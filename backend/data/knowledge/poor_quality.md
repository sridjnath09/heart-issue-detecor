# Poor-quality recordings
Source: recording guidance (educational).
Noise from breathing, speech, clothing friction and environmental sounds can mimic or mask murmurs. Re-record in a quiet room, with the stethoscope or microphone firmly on bare skin, while holding breath briefly, for at least 10-20 seconds, at the apex, left sternal edge, and right/left upper sternal border. Results from low-quality recordings should not be relied on.
