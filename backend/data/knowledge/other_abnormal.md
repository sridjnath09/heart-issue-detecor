# Other abnormal heart sounds
Source: general auscultation teaching summary (educational).
Includes extra sounds (S3, S4, clicks, snaps, rubs) and irregular rhythms that do not match a specific pattern. An S3 can reflect volume overload or heart failure in adults; an S4 reflects a stiff ventricle. A pericardial friction rub is a scratchy sound suggesting pericarditis. Because the pattern is non-specific, clinical examination plus ECG and echocardiography are recommended to determine the cause.
