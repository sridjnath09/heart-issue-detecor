# Mitral valve prolapse (MVP)
Source: general cardiology summary (educational).
Mitral leaflets bulge into the left atrium in systole. Classic finding: mid-systolic click, sometimes followed by a late systolic murmur. Often benign, but can progress to mitral regurgitation, and rarely associates with arrhythmia. Symptoms may include palpitations or atypical chest pain. Next steps: echocardiography to confirm and assess for regurgitation; periodic follow-up; ECG or ambulatory monitoring if palpitations. Most people need no treatment beyond surveillance.
