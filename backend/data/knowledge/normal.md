# Normal heart sounds
Source: general auscultation teaching summary (educational).
A normal recording shows a regular S1 (mitral/tricuspid closure) and S2 (aortic/pulmonic closure) with silent systole and diastole. Resting adult rate is typically 60-100 bpm; children are faster. Slight rate variation with breathing (sinus arrhythmia) is normal, especially in young people. Innocent (functional) flow murmurs are soft, short, systolic, and common in children and during fever, anaemia, pregnancy or exercise. Next step: routine care; re-record in a quiet room if signal quality is poor.
