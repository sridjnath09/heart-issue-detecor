# Heart murmur (general)
Source: general auscultation teaching summary; see 2020 ACC/AHA Valvular Heart Disease Guideline for management.
A murmur is an extra sound from turbulent blood flow. Timing (systolic/diastolic/continuous), intensity (grade 1-6), pitch and location help distinguish innocent from pathological murmurs. Features favouring pathology: diastolic timing, loud (>=grade 3) or harsh murmurs, symptoms (breathlessness, chest pain, syncope, palpitations), abnormal S2, ejection click, or murmur radiating to the neck or axilla. Recommended next step: clinical evaluation by a physician; transthoracic echocardiography is the standard test to confirm valve disease and quantify severity. Urgent assessment if symptoms such as fainting, chest pain or severe breathlessness are present.
